# 🇬🇧 Post (English — story version)

A few months ago I was handed a job I didn't see coming: arrange the seating
for a family wedding. 🪑

Hundreds of guests. Who sits next to whom. Which families can't be split up.
Who confirmed, who didn't. And the "system" for all of it? A stack of paper, a
pen, and a lot of crossing things out.

So I went looking for an app to make it easier — and couldn't find one that
actually fit how our weddings work. So I built it.

Meet **GuestSeat** 🎉

→ Drag guests onto tables, with a capacity limit per table
→ Link couples & families so they never get seated apart
→ Auto-seat the whole list in one click
→ Track RSVP, meals and a live overview at a glance
→ Build the invitation (with valle, tupanat, sofra…) and export a print-ready PDF
→ Share the entire plan with a QR code — no server, the whole list lives in the link
→ Works fully offline, in your browser, in English & Albanian

The age of paper seating charts is over.

It's free and runs entirely in the browser 👉 [add your link]

Built with React + TypeScript. I'd genuinely love your feedback. 🙌
